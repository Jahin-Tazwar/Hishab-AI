title: Documentary evidence for input tax credit
topic_tags: [itc, documentary_evidence, mushak_6_3]

No input tax credit shall be allowed unless the registered person claiming
the credit is in possession of, and produces upon demand, the documentary
evidence prescribed under this Act and the rules made thereunder. For the
purposes of this section the prescribed documentary evidence shall include:

(a) the tax invoice issued by the supplier in the form specified in the
rules (Mushak 6.3 or such other form as may be notified);
(b) bill of entry, treasury challan, or other prescribed document evidencing
payment of value added tax at the import stage;
(c) the books of account and records required to be maintained under this
Act in which the relevant supply has been duly recorded.

The documentary evidence shall correspond, as to taxpayer particulars,
invoice number, date, taxable value and tax amount, with the records of
the issuing supplier; any material discrepancy may, after due opportunity,
result in disallowance of the credit.
