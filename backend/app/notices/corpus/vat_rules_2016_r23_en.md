title: Procedure for taking input tax credit
topic_tags: [itc, procedure]
---

A registered person who is entitled to take input tax credit under Section
46 of the Act shall take such credit in the manner provided in this rule:

(a) the credit shall be claimed in the return filed for the tax period in
which the tax invoice or other prescribed document is received, or in
a subsequent return filed within the period prescribed under these
rules;
(b) the registered person shall enter the particulars of each supply on
which input tax credit is claimed in the purchase register and in the
return, in the manner specified in the form thereof;
(c) where the supply is received in one tax period but the prescribed
document is received in a later tax period, the credit may be claimed
in the period in which the document is received, subject to the time
limit under these rules.

Input tax credit shall not be carried forward beyond the period prescribed
under these rules, and any credit not claimed within that period shall
lapse.
