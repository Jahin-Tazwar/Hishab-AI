title: Resolution of input tax credit mismatches
topic_tags: [mismatch, reconciliation, procedure]
---

Where, on examination of the returns and supporting records, a mismatch is
observed between the input tax credit claimed by a registered person and
the corresponding output tax declared by the supplier, the following
procedure shall be followed for resolution:

(a) the appropriate officer shall, in writing, intimate the mismatch to
the registered person, specifying the invoice particulars, the period,
the amount of credit in question and the apparent reason for the
mismatch;
(b) the registered person shall, within the time specified in the
intimation, submit a reconciliation accompanied by the relevant tax
invoice, supplier confirmation, proof of payment, and any other
evidence relied upon;
(c) where the registered person establishes that the credit was properly
claimed and is supported by valid documentary evidence under Rule 24
and Section 49 of the Act, the credit shall stand allowed and the
intimation shall be closed;
(d) where the mismatch is not satisfactorily reconciled within the
specified time, the credit may be disallowed in accordance with
Section 50 of the Act after affording an opportunity of hearing.

A reasonable opportunity to rectify clerical or typographical mismatches
shall be afforded before any disallowance is ordered.
