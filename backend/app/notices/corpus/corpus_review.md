# Citation corpus — provenance audit checklist

The V1 corpus body text is **DRAFT** — paraphrased summaries of each clause
written from public knowledge of Bangladesh's VAT and SD Act 2012 and Rules
2016. Before this corpus is used to ground production drafts, a Bangladeshi
CA must audit each row against the NBR-published official Bangla and English
texts and either:

1. Mark `status = REVIEWED` if the draft text faithfully captures the clause, or
2. Replace the body with the verbatim official text and then mark `REVIEWED`.
