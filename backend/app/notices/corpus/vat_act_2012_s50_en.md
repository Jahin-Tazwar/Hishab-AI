title: Grounds for denial of input tax credit
topic_tags: [itc, denial_grounds, mismatch]
---

Notwithstanding any provision permitting input tax credit, the credit
claimed by a registered person shall be disallowed, in whole or in part,
where the appropriate authority is satisfied that:

(a) the supply in respect of which the credit is claimed is not supported
by valid documentary evidence as required under this Act and the rules;
(b) the particulars declared by the claimant do not match those declared by
the supplier in the supplier's return, and the discrepancy is not
satisfactorily reconciled;
(c) the supply, or any portion thereof, was not in fact received or was not
used in the course or furtherance of a taxable economic activity;
(d) the supply falls within a class in respect of which input tax credit is
expressly disallowed by this Act or any order issued thereunder.

Before disallowing any credit under this section the appropriate authority
shall afford the registered person a reasonable opportunity of being heard
and of producing evidence in support of the claim.
