title: Conditions for input tax credit
topic_tags: [itc, eligibility, documentary_evidence, mismatch]

A registered person shall be entitled to take an input tax credit in respect
of value added tax paid on the acquisition of taxable supplies used, or to be
used, in the course or furtherance of an economic activity that makes taxable
supplies, subject to the following conditions:

(a) the supply is acquired from a registered person;
(b) the registered person holds the documentary evidence prescribed under
this Act and the rules made thereunder, including a valid tax invoice
issued in respect of the supply;
(c) the input tax credit is taken within the period prescribed by the rules;
(d) the supply is not of a class or description in respect of which input
tax credit is expressly disallowed under this Act or any order issued
thereunder.

Where any of the foregoing conditions is not satisfied, the input tax credit
claimed shall be liable to be disallowed and recovered together with such
interest and penalty as may be applicable.
