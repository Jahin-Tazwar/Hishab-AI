title: Assessment and demand for unpaid VAT
topic_tags: [assessment, demand, procedure]
---

Where the appropriate officer has reason to believe that a registered person
has failed to pay value added tax that is properly payable, or has wrongly
claimed any input tax credit, drawback, or refund, the officer may, after
giving the person a show-cause notice specifying the grounds and the amount
in question, determine the value added tax payable and issue a demand for
recovery thereof together with applicable interest and penalty.

The show-cause notice shall state:
(a) the period to which the alleged short-payment or wrongful claim relates;
(b) the amount of value added tax, interest and penalty proposed to be
demanded; and
(c) the time, not being less than the period prescribed by the rules,
within which the registered person may submit a written reply and
documentary evidence in defence of the claim.

An order under this section shall be made in writing after considering the
reply and any evidence produced, and shall be communicated to the registered
person along with reasons.
