title: Documentary evidence required to support input tax credit
topic_tags: [itc, documentary_evidence]
---

For the purposes of Section 49 of the Act, the documentary evidence required
to support a claim of input tax credit shall, according to the nature of the
supply, include:

(a) in the case of a local taxable supply, the original tax invoice issued
by the supplier in the prescribed form (Mushak 6.3), containing the
supplier's and recipient's particulars, the description and value of
the supply, and the value added tax charged;
(b) in the case of an imported supply, the bill of entry endorsed by
customs together with the treasury challan evidencing payment of value
added tax at the import stage;
(c) in the case of supplies received under withholding arrangements, the
withholding certificate in the prescribed form together with proof of
deposit of the withheld tax to the government treasury.

The above documents shall be retained by the registered person for the
period prescribed under these rules and produced before the appropriate
officer upon demand. Where the documents do not match the supplier's
declared particulars, the credit may be disallowed in accordance with
Section 50 of the Act.
